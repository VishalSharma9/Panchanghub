Classical Yoga Categories Explained

Pancha Mahapurusha Yogas Formed when non-luminaries (Mars, Mercury, Jupiter, Venus, Saturn) occupy Kendras in own or exaltation signs. Bestows peerless personal, professional, or ethical distinction.
Raja Yogas Dynamic partnerships between Kendra lords (action, pillar houses) and Trikona lords (dharma, grace). Grants sovereign leadership, professional authority, and enduring prosperity.
Viparita Raja Yogas Formed by lords of Dusthana houses (6th, 8th, 12th) situated exclusively in other Dusthana houses without benefic contamination. Converts sudden crisis into unexpected triumph.
Dhana Yogas Interconnections linking the 2nd (treasury), 5th (speculative intellect), 9th (fortune), and 11th (gains) houses. Generates enduring material abundance and financial resilience.
Solar & Lunar Yogas Planetary placements flanking the Sun (Vesi, Vosi, Ubhayachari) or Moon (Sunapha, Anapha, Durudhura, Gaja Kesari, Adhi Yoga), molding constitutional vigor and emotional magnetism.
Knowledge & Renunciation Yogas Saraswati, Budhaditya, and Pravrajya yogas cultivating supreme literary mastery, analytical genius, public honor, and spiritual detachment.
