How to read this chart

This is a tropical chart. The signs are the seasons, not the sidereal signs used on the Vedic pages. The Ascendant ({{ c.ascendant.sign }}) is the sign rising at birth. It is the way you enter a room, and it is the start of the houses. The Midheaven ({{ c.midheaven.sign }}) is the public point: work, reputation, what the life is seen as.

A planet in a sign is the style. A planet in a house is the room of life where that style shows up. Retrograde (Rx) means the planet was moving backward in the sky. The theme turns inward. It is not a defect.

An aspect is a conversation between two planets. A conjunction blends them. An opposition pulls in two directions and needs both. A square is friction that forces a decision. A trine is ease, which can also be laziness. A sextile is a small opening you still have to use.
